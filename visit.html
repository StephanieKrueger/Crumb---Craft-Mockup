Pre-Order & Market FAQ

Everything you need to know about grabbing your goods

How do online pre-orders work?

Simply select your favorite loaves on our shop page, check out online, and choose your preferred pickup morning. Just show your confirmation text at Stall #12!

Do you accept walk-ups?

Yes! We always save a portion of each batch for walk-up visitors, though our specialty items tend to sell out quickly on weekend mornings.

Are your loaves sliced?

To preserve freshness, moisture, and crisp crusts, we keep all our sourdough loaves whole. Feel free to ask if you'd like us to slice it at the stand!

Can I order for private events?

We love handling custom orders for weddings, local cafes, and dinner parties! Shoot us an email or reach out on Instagram to chat about custom bakes.
